## 📈 Project Overview
This repository contains an interactive sales and operations dashboard built to transform raw retail data into actionable business strategic decisions. The dashboard visualizes key retail metrics, identifying high-performing products, seasonal trends, and regional sales distribution to drive profitability.

### 🧠 Key Features & Insights
* **Executive Summary Metrics:** Real-time tracking of Total Sales, Net Profit ($85,070), Profit Margin (29.6%), and Year-over-Year (YoY) growth trends.
* **Geographical Performance:** Regional analysis highlighting Egypt as the top-performing market ($86,027.88), followed closely by Iraq.
* **Product Profitability Analysis:** Clear optimization visibility showing *Predator Freak* as the highest profitable product line.
* **Distribution Channel Insights:** Breakdown of fulfillment performance showing the Online Store leading sales volume (45%) compared to traditional Wholesale (7%).
* **Advanced Analytics:** Integrated monthly trend lines and a Pareto (80/20 rule) cumulative distribution analysis for inventory optimization.
